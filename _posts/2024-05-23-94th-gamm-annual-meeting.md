The president and vice president of the GAMM Student Chapter Ulm participated in the GAMM Annual Meeting 2024. 

The meeting took place from March 18 to March 22 at the Otto von Guericke University of Magdeburg. During the meeting, the two of them had the opportunity to present some results from their research. Moreover, the conference also provided an opportunity to exchange ideas with other GAMM Student Chapters from other cities in Germany.
